# 4PIE Dashboard report content template

Use this as the content inventory for each new report. Omit unsupported modules; never fill them with generic prose.

## Packet-backed front matter

- Cover identity, birth data, generated date, four-school label, core thesis.
- `consequential_judgments`: five ranked conclusions with tier, event certainty and main years.
- `school_role_manifest`: four run-specific method cards.
- `consensus_matrix`: 6-10 material questions, not generic domains alone.

## Life-stage timeline

For each stage: age/years, theme, main task, favourable version, risk version, evidence claim IDs. State what changes from the prior stage.

## Career dashboard

- Core occupational function and selected alternative.
- Three strongest abilities supported by chart evidence.
- High-match, conditional-match and poor-match environments.
- Route: skill -> demonstrable output -> platform/client -> authority -> professional position.
- Five job-choice questions grounded in the adjudication.
- Four-school evidence strip, validation questions, and timing.

## Wealth dashboard

- Income channels: fixed income, professional service, output/content, side business, collaboration, investment; show only supported channels.
- Retention drains: distinguish fixed cost, learning, relocation, equipment, shared expense, or new ventures.
- Asset stage: cash reserve, income expansion, investment, property, long-term obligation.
- Explicit downside conditions and prohibited leaps.

## Relationship dashboard

- Attraction -> trust -> commitment, with different conditions and speeds.
- Partner or relationship environment selected over alternatives.
- Pressure carriers: distance, work, time, family, city, shared money.
- Six-month reality checks and relationship timing.

## Home, movement and family

- Residence function, relocation direction, family duty, fixed assets and their competing branches.
- Separate desire to move, permission to move, and a timed carrier for moving.

## Annual roadmap

For each year: central task, career, wealth, relationship, residence/mobility, opportunity, downside, action, carry-in, result preparing the next year. Follow with no more than five ranked three-layer timing windows.

## Decision tools

Split dense content into two pages:

1. Career and development: first long-term role, job change, side business, relocation, study.
2. Relationship and assets: commitment, cohabitation, investment, property, partner/collaborator.

Columns: decision, favourable choice, warning, consensus tier, event certainty, main time, next action.

## Ninety-day action page

- Now to day 30: at most three actions.
- Day 30-60: at most three tests or outputs.
- Day 60-90: decisions to continue, expand or stop.

Every action must trace to an approved claim. Label it as advice.

# English Reader policy

Write idiomatic international English directly from the approved claims.

- Use plain sentences before technical terminology.
- Keep Bazi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Western astrology and Vedic astrology distinct.
- Preserve claim meaning, selected/rejected versions, scores, dates and uncertainty.
- Use `relationship`, `career`, `wealth`, `home and family`, `network and reputation`,
  `learning`, `stress and recovery`, and `authority and status` consistently.
- Explain school-specific terms on first use; do not translate star names into invented equivalents.
- Avoid therapy language, motivational filler, fortune-cookie prose and guaranteed events.
- Use ISO dates in technical sections and readable month names in prose.
- State that the report is reflective/entertainment content, not medical, legal or financial advice.

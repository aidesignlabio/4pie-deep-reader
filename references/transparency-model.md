# Transparent four-school adjudication

## School roles

- `western`: behavioural mechanisms, house-ruler chains, aspect networks, natal capacities, and modern transit activation.
- `ziwei`: life-area structure, palace networks, natal/decadal/annual hierarchy, and verified transformations.
- `vedic`: functional lords, strength measures, stable divisional evidence, dashas, and major transits.
- `bazi`: seasonal structure, stems/branches, ten-god dynamics, and luck-pillar activation. When strength, climate, or useful-element decisions are unresolved, do not use them directionally.

No school must speak on every question. Use `not_applicable` when its method does not answer the question, and `insufficient` when required data is absent or unstable.

## Position vocabulary

- `support`: independently reaches materially the same answer for the same question and time layer.
- `refine`: accepts the direction and adds a condition, stage, or narrower manifestation.
- `limit`: restricts its strength, duration, or favourable realization.
- `oppose`: answers the same question and time layer with an outcome that cannot coexist with the proposed answer.
- `not_comparable`: addresses another layer, stage, or kind of outcome.
- `not_applicable`: no relevant method for this question.
- `insufficient`: relevant calculation or rule is missing or unstable.

`oppose` is valid only when the schools answer the same question, at the same time layer, and the outcomes cannot both be true under different conditions.

## Consensus tiers

Consensus is an audit label, never a vote or probability.

- `high_consensus`: at least three independently derived, applicable positions converge; no verified opposition remains; evidence chains are complete.
- `moderate_consensus`: two independent applicable positions converge and the others refine, limit, are not comparable, or are unavailable.
- `single_school_strong_signal`: one school has a complete, method-relevant chain without independent confirmation.
- `conflict`: verified applicable schools reach incompatible answers. Preserve both branches and state what can resolve them.
- `insufficient`: no complete chain selects a direction.

Do not upgrade a conclusion because several schools mention similar words. Independence, method relevance, stability, derivation completeness, and exclusion of alternatives matter more than count.

## Two confidence axes

- `consensus_strength`: convergence on the structural judgment.
- `event_certainty`: timing support and availability of a real-world carrier.

A natal theme may have high consensus and low event certainty. Never convert either axis into a percentage.

## Statement layers

Keep these functions visibly separate:

1. `model_judgment`: what verified chart evidence selects.
2. `reality_projection`: concrete life versions it may produce; never claim these were observed.
3. `action_guidance`: choices that improve the branch; advice is not evidence.
4. `validation_condition`: an observable result that supports, downgrades, or overturns the judgment.

## Evidence chain

Every major claim requires a stable `claim_id`, exact question and time layer, four school positions, source fact IDs or evidence paths, short native reasoning, selected and rejected alternatives, consensus tier and reason, unresolved conflict, and separate event certainty when timing is involved.

The Reader may compress this into a small evidence box, but it may not hide all school positions in an appendix.

## Timing layers

1. `background_period`: long-period climate permitting a class of outcomes.
2. `sensitivity_window`: narrower interval when a domain is unusually active or decidable.
3. `event_carrier`: external opportunity, decision, person, contract, vacancy, move, payment, examination, or other channel required for manifestation.

Exact cycle changes are `transition_dates`, not event dates. Each carries `not_event_date: true`. An absent carrier lowers event certainty even when the background and sensitivity window remain valid.

# Bazi L1 adjudication policy

## Purpose

L1 consumes deterministic Bazi calculation output and produces auditable native judgments. It never recalculates the four pillars from prose and never treats an element count as a strength percentage.

## Required inputs

- four pillars, day master, month command and seasonal status;
- hidden stems with qi type and table weight;
- day-master roots and visible/hidden support;
- stem and branch relations with transformation status;
- structure candidates and failure conditions;
- climate status;
- ranked useful-element candidates with status;
- luck-pillar boundaries and annual pillars.

If any required strength input is absent, set `strength_decision.status=insufficient`. A conditional structure or useful-element list may not be printed as a settled fact.

## Strength decision

Use ordered evidence, not addition alone:

1. month command and seasonal authority;
2. root existence, qi quality and whether the root is attacked;
3. visible stems and rootedness of印比;
4. opposing 财官杀食伤 and whether they are rooted or seasonally active;
5. combinations only when transformation is confirmed;
6. climate as a separate axis.

`身弱有根有助` is verified only when the day master loses the command, has at least one real root, and has visible or hidden 印比 support. It rejects follow structures but does not imply that support outweighs opposition.

## Structure decision

- The month-command ten god creates a candidate, not an automatic finished structure.
- `七殺格` requires an explicit judgment of control or transformation:食神制殺, 傷官制殺, or印化殺.
- When a controlling or transforming star exists but adequacy is not demonstrated, use `conditional_structure`.
- State the competing chain, such as財生殺, and whether its components are rooted, seasonal, or only visible.
- Never label a mixed chart as pure or completed without exclusion tests.

## Useful elements

Keep three questions separate:

- `support`: what helps the day master bear the structure;
- `mediation`: what connects or controls competing forces;
- `climate`: what adjusts cold, heat, dryness or dampness.

For a weak Earth day master under spring Wood pressure, Fire may be a support/mediation candidate and Earth a root/support candidate. Metal is conditional because output can control Wood while also draining Earth. These are directional candidates, not universal lucky elements.

## Luck and annual activation

For each period record:

- added ten god and element;
- support or drain relative to the verified strength decision;
- clashes, combinations, punishments and harms involving natal pillars and the active luck pillar;
- favourable mechanism, adverse mechanism and net label `supportive|mixed|pressurising|structural_change`.

An annual branch repeating a natal branch is not automatically favourable. Exact dates remain solar-term boundaries, not event dates.

## Domain translation

L1 may enter cross-school adjudication only through bounded positions:

- career/status: structure,官杀,印,食伤 and luck activation;
- wealth:财 strength versus bearing and retention conditions;
- relationship: spouse-star and day-branch structure, without guaranteeing partner quality or marriage date;
- home/family: pillar/luck interactions only as a modifier; never infer property from an element alone;
- timing: annual structural pressure/support; never infer a specific event without a carrier.

Every domain position must carry `support|refine|limit|oppose|not_comparable|insufficient`, evidence IDs, a limitation, and a failure rule.
